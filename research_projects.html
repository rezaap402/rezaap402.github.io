Current research projects

Detection of Porang Flour Adulteration

This project began in 2023 and investigates the performance of Vis–NIR and NIR spectroscopy for estimating the quality parameters of porang flour. In the initial study, partial least squares regression combined with variable importance in projection was used to identify the most informative spectral regions, enabling moisture content prediction using NIR spectroscopy. The research was subsequently extended to glucomannan content prediction using the full NIR spectral range. Least absolute shrinkage and selection operator regression was applied to select twelve informative wavelengths, which were then coupled with Gaussian process regression. For NIR hyperspectral imaging, Dr. Hanim Zuhrotul Amanah of Universitas Gadjah Mada, Indonesia, initiated an international collaborative project involving Chungbuk National University and Chungnam National University, Republic of Korea. This collaboration focuses on the nondestructive detection and quantification of adulteration in porang flour.

Chilling-Stress Detection in Pepper Plants

This project explores high-throughput identification of chilling stress in pepper plants using fluorescence multispectral imaging. Explainable machine-learning methods are used to identify important spectral features and interpret plant stress responses.
